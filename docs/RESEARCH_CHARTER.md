# Research Charter — PhiBridge369 v0.1

**Status:** Speculative research program with a buildable first demonstration.

## Core question

Can an existing intelligent agent voluntarily control, perceive through, and perhaps someday inhabit an alternate physical substrate? What would distinguish behavioral similarity from subjective continuity of the same experiencer?

No reproducible experiment currently demonstrates that a human's subjective consciousness has been transferred into a computer or robotic vessel. PhiBridge369 does not claim otherwise.

## Definitions

- **Telepresence:** A person controls a remote or virtual body while remaining the original biological participant.
- **Cognitive model:** Software representing selected characteristics from voluntarily provided data.
- **Copy or emulation:** A second implementation might reproduce behavior or memories without demonstrating sameness of subjective experience.
- **Continuity:** The unresolved claim that the same first-person experiencer persists through a substrate change.
- **Native synthetic occupant:** A hypothetical or future agent originating within computation rather than copied from a human; consciousness cannot be inferred from appearance.

## Research tracks

1. Embodiment: sensor-to-action mapping, voluntary control, feedback, virtual bodies, and guarded telepresence.
2. Information representation: auditable handling of identity-related data with minimization and deletion protections if ever collected.
3. Continuity hypotheses: compare copy, gradual replacement, overlap, and signal-anchor models. Mark speculative claims clearly.
4. Occupant autonomy: support the intended occupant's input into physical form, capabilities, and operating boundaries, constrained by safety.
5. Falsifiability: define measurements and alternatives, not just compelling demos.

## R01 hypothesis

A browser-only state machine can demonstrate the crucial difference between connection, authorization, control, and feedback. Its constraints can be specified and tested without any implied mind migration.

## Evidence standard

Log the command, its permission gate, the simulated action, and the feedback separately. Test that a stopped session never silently reconnects or restores revoked permissions.

## Non-goals

- Immortality claims or medical treatments.
- Destructive brain experiments or involuntary procedures.
- Claims of hidden successful mind transfer.
- Declaring a simulated humanoid conscious without evidence.
- Treating an application stop control as a physical safety circuit.

**North star:** People Above Profit. Autonomy Above Ownership. Evidence Above Assumption.
